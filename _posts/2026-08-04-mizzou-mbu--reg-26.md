# Mizzou Merit Badge University Registration

The Mizzou Merit Badge University is a great local opportunity for scouts to potentially earn 2 merit badges. The number of merit badges offered this year is fewer than previous years, therefore we want to register as early as possible to get first choice for merit badges, and registration opens soon, 8/15! So let me know if your scout plans to go and see the notes below about picking merit badges. 

* Date: 10/24/2026 all day
    * Registration opens 8/15/26
* Cost: $27 
* Notes
    * There are a fair number of Eagle required merit badges offered, do check them out, but do be aware they may require prerequisites that your scout may, or may not, be able to meet. 
    * Here is a link to the merit badge catalog: [PDF](https://scoutingevent.com/653-MIZZOU2026#)
        * Have your scout pick a morning and afternoon merit badge and a back up choice if the class fills up
        * Some merit badge classes are morning and afternoon
